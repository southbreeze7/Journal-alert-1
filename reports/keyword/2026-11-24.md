- **匹配**：keyword（标题：MASH） ｜ **得分**：9

> Hepatic stellate cells (HSCs) are key drivers of fibrosis in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH). Hepatocyte expression of Jagged1, a critical Notch ligand, is increased in patients with MASH-associated fibrosis, and is necessary to induce fibrosis in mice, attributed to inter-hepatocyte Notch signaling. As HSC Notch activity is also increased in patients and mouse models of MASH, we investigated whether hepatocyte to HSC Notch signaling provides a parallel and additive path to liver fibrosis.
